### H29. A FALSY PARAMETER DOES NOT REACH THE EXPERT. Verified with an echo expert

**Found by the AGP team (our first external user), verified by us on 17 Aug 2026** with an
echo expert that returns the `repr()` of what it received:

| sent | arrived at the expert |
|---|---|
| `False`, `0`, `""` | **the default value** — all three |
| `True`, `7`, `"text"` | as sent |
| `"False"`, `"0"` as strings | as sent |
| `" "` (a single space) | **the default value** |
| nothing sent | the default value |

**1. False and zero are indistinguishable from "wasn't passed."** The expert has no way
to know the person explicitly said "no": it sees its own default. Hence the rule —
**safe behavior must be the default value, and turning on something dangerous must go
through a truthy value.** A flag `confirmed=False` does not protect; `confirmed="yes"`
does.

**2. A string made of nothing but spaces also disappears** — beyond AGP's finding. A
space as a meaningful separator cannot be passed as a parameter.

**How to catch this yourself.** A five-line echo expert that returns the `repr()` of each
parameter is the fastest contract check that exists.
