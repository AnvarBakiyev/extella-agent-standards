# H84. Acceptance of a windowed product must click through the client surfaces

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H84. Acceptance of a windowed product must click through the client surfaces

The Odoo builder product passed `ready → restart → ready` and all 71 checks, and at the buyer's
the apps-menu button hung the window and produced a gray screen (measured 04 Sep 2026: four
crash reports `Extella Helper (Renderer)`, `EXC_BREAKPOINT / SIGTRAP`). At those moments Odoo,
the database, and the gateway were **healthy**, with **no** 4xx/5xx errors.

The cause belongs to a class, not a single product: the tile-menu button draws a **client-side
overlay with no server call**. So the health check and the `ready → restart → ready` acceptance
**cannot see it by construction** — the server answers, and the renderer crashes. A renderer
crash is not an HTTP error.

**Rule.** For a windowed product, acceptance must go over the **client surfaces**, not just
server responses: open the apps menu, open every root app, touch overlays and modals, close and
reopen. The sign of failure is a gray/white frame, a hung DOM, or a fresh renderer crash report
with a healthy server and zero 5xx. This is the same requirement as H63 (a product with no
hooks is unverifiable by a machine), but for surfaces that never touch the server at all: their
check is replaced by neither the health check nor `ready → restart → ready`.

An honesty caveat: a full machine check of the real window cannot be assembled today — it runs
into Extella Desktop's lack of renderer-crash hooks and a test mode (an item for the platform).
Until they exist, a human goes through the client surfaces on a short list, a minute's worth,
not "click through everything."
