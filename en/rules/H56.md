# H56. Someone else's envelope: one expert's response format applied to another

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H56. Someone else's envelope: one expert's response format applied to another

**Probe from the same chat, 18 Aug 2026.** The analysis applied the shared envelope of
neighboring experts to the intermediary's response and looked for the nested result
field. The panel decided the channel was inactive and offered a repair, even though the
processing had answered about sixteen seconds earlier.

**What to do.** The response format is specific to each expert and is parsed by its own
contract: the canonical response is read from the root, without expecting someone
else's wrapper. The contract is pinned down by a structural check, not by assumptions
based on a neighbor.

Related to H40 from the other side: there the wrapper was stripped too eagerly and
domain data was lost, here the wrapper was looked for where there wasn't one, and a live
channel was declared dead. It's the same mistake — the response format was taken from
memory, not from the contract.
