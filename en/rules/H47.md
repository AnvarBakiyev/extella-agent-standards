### H47. BEFORE ROLLING OUT, CHECK THE SNAPSHOT'S EXPERT COUNT

**Log of the "1C Agent" chat, 20 Aug 2026.** An outside expert (the claude.ai bridge)
had self-written itself into the local scope of the storefront's source agent — and would
have shipped to every buyer inside the version snapshot. The only visible trace was the
counter in `add-version`'s stream progress: `total: 7` instead of the expected 6.

This is an internals leak to buyers, and it's silent: nothing crashes. The rule: before
every rollout, check the snapshot's expert count against the product's expected
composition. Delete the extra one from the local copy (`/api/expert/remove`,
`global:false` — the global copy stays intact) and re-cut the version. Related to the
silent MCP `save_expert` duplicate that writes into someone else's scope.
