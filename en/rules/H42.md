# H42. A delete responds with success and doesn't delete

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H42. A delete responds with success and doesn't delete

**Probed by the bridges chat, 16 Aug 2026.** Deleting a rule returns a success code and
the body `{"status":"success","deleted":false}` — the rule remains in place regardless.
Through MCP, the same call returns `false`. The desktop wrapper, on top of that, was
swallowing the refusal and sending the page a success flag.

**What to do.** Delete from the creator's own scope. Treat `deleted:false` as an error,
not as information: the word about success refers to the request, not to the
consequence. Re-read the list after any edit. The wrapper is forbidden from reporting
success to the page until the object's absence has been confirmed by a re-read (this is a
specific case of H38-P, point 1).
