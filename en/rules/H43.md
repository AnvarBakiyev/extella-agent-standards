# H43. A shared expert: `global` gives visibility, but does not carry execution over

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/en/rules/INDEX.md).

### H43. A shared expert: `global` gives visibility, but does not carry execution over

**Probed by the bridges chat; re-verified here on 21 Aug 2026 — and the result diverged.**

The original finding: `save_expert` with the shared-access flag does not guarantee
execution from every scope. Searching and reading find the expert from different scopes,
but running it only works from the one that saved the record last; saving it again moves
availability to the new scope. The launch device and the access code have no bearing on
this.

**A repeat probe gives a different result.** An expert saved once with the shared-access
flag ran from three different scopes in a row, and all three answered with success. So
the refusal doesn't always reproduce — it happens on a NAME COLLISION: when the same name
is saved from several scopes, whichever saved last wins. A single copy of a single name
works from everywhere.

**What to do.** Don't rely on the shared flag as a way to hand out execution rights, and
keep the name unique across the account. The measured workaround for a name collision is
a separate copy in each calling scope with a character-by-character comparison; it was
not rolled out into the product because of the risk of the copies drifting apart.

**Why the results diverged matters more than the finding itself.** Two probes contradict
each other, and the rule is recorded not as "it works" or "it doesn't work," but with a
named condition. A rule with no condition here would have been harmful either way: one
version would force copies to be made where they aren't needed, the other would leave the
product with a broken launch.
