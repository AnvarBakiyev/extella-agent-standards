### H4. The sandbox: what's allowed and what isn't (verified by a probe)

The page opens in an `iframe sandbox="allow-scripts allow-forms allow-popups
allow-modals allow-downloads"` — **without** `allow-same-origin`. Consequences:

* **allowed**: calling the OS's own API — its CORS is explicitly set to `Origin: null` —
  verified, `access-control-allow-origin: null` comes back and `x-extella-token` is
  permitted;
* **not allowed**: reaching the desktop, `localStorage`, cookies, or the environment's
  token;
* **not allowed**: reaching `localhost` — if local data is needed, the product is a
  device-type one (sections B–G);
* **extensions are not injected into the sandbox** — they only work for ordinary sites in
  the OS's browser. (Meaning our shell extension from 10 Aug is not applicable to
  page-type products in principle.)
