# H64. An app's scaffold is taken ready-made, not drawn from scratch

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H64. An app's scaffold is taken ready-made, not drawn from scratch

A new app starts from a copy of the working scaffold, which already has the form,
result output, four states (empty, waiting, error, ready), a bridge to the platform with
a whitelist of experts and a single timeout, a local mode for checking the interface
without an expert, and `data-testid` hooks.

The point of the rule is not "saving time" but sameness: a product assembled from the
scaffold passes acceptance by construction, while one drawn from scratch each time
brings its own opening defects.

The scaffold lives at `templates/app-recipe/` in this repository: the panel, the
bridge, the build, quick checks, the acceptance checklist, and the panel's own
auto-click-through.

Cross-check on 23 Aug 2026: the colors and fonts of the ready scaffold matched the
design section in `AGENT_BUILD_GUIDE` — no discrepancies, so a second copy of the
palette is deliberately not given here (two copies of the palette have already once
drifted apart).
