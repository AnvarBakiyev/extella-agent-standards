# H99. ZERO IS A CLAIM THAT "THIS IS EMPTY," NOT "I DON'T KNOW"

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H99. ZERO IS A CLAIM THAT "THIS IS EMPTY," NOT "I DON'T KNOW"

**Source: the 1C Agent chat, an interview on 21 Sep 2026. He called this the one prohibition he
would put into the canon for every Extella product.**

**Measurement.** The "Debts by age" screen showed a total of **16,492,475 ₸** and, next to it,
four zero buckets by age. Everything was computed correctly: the settlements document isn't
filled in the database, there's nowhere to take the age from. Next to it stood the caption
"over 90 days — none."

**Cost.** A person sees real money and zeros and decides the product is broken. And "over 90
days — none" is a **plain untruth**: when the age is unknown, there is nothing to claim. An
accountant who believes that phrase won't go deal with overdue receivables. This isn't
cosmetics, it's the cost of a decision.

**Rule.** It is forbidden to show a computed value where there was nothing to compute from, and
forbidden to state a negation about something the product never checked. If it wasn't computed,
name the amount and the reason in words, remove the empty breakdowns, don't phrase a negation.
Here's how it should sound in production:

> Nothing to break down by age: 16,492,475 ₸. For not a single amount does 1C hold a
> settlements document — either "settlements by document" wasn't enabled in the contract card,
> or the debt was carried over during the move to 1C. The total and the breakdown by
> counterparty are correct; there's nothing to compute the age from.

**Check for any new screen:** what will it show if the client is missing the needed field? The
answer "zeros" — redo it. Pin it down with a test, not a memo: memos get forgotten on the very
first rushed release.
