# H88. Patches go in as raw strings, with dedup and a check afterward

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H88. Patches go in as raw strings, with dedup and a check afterward

A recurring time-loss across several chats: `\n` in f-string heredocs collapses, slice patches
spawn duplicate definitions (the last one wins), and "the fix didn't apply." A mass `replace`
once hit a line inside a function's body and set off recursion.

**Rule.** Write patches as raw strings (`r'...'`); after patching, verify: `python -c "import
ast; ast.parse(open(...).read())"` and `grep -c 'def '` (there should be no duplicate names).
Check a mass replacement for landing inside a function body. "The fix didn't apply" almost
always means a duplicate or a collapsed line break, not a stubborn bug.
