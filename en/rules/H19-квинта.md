### H19-quinta. CLOSED 14 Aug 2026: both deleting your own clutter and removing an extension

The platform fixed two things the same day, and both lift our limitations:

**1. `allowed_origins` is saved, the forwarding proxy works.** Measurement: the field is written
and read back correctly, `ext/fetch` to an allowed address returned content in **1.8 seconds**, a
disallowed address is still `403`. So the "page → someone else's API" path is open — and it's
**seven times faster** than reaching the device (1.8 s versus 13–17 s).

**2. Your own listing can be deleted even if the author himself bought it.** The earlier refusal,
"1 buyer(s) purchased this app," applied to your own test purchase. Measurement: **11 out of 11
junk listings** were deleted, zero refusals.

**This also closes the question of extensions**, which I had declared unsolvable: an extension is
removed by **deleting its listing** — the record leaves the account library along with the
listing. After that, the desktop entry can be removed by editing state and **does not come back**
(verified with pauses of 10 and 30 seconds). The rule from H19-bis still holds: editing desktop
state only sticks if the record is gone from the library.
