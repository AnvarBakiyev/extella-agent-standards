# H23. STOREFRONT CARD: icon, description, tags — at the moment of rollout, not "later"

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H23. STOREFRONT CARD: icon, description, tags — at the moment of rollout, not "later"

**Census of 14 Aug 2026 across our ten listings:**

    no icon        — 4 of 10
    no description — 3 of 10 ("1С Agent AI", "Telecom Demo", "Lawyer" — name only)
    name with a version and service words — does occur

This isn't about looking nice. The icon is the only thing by which a person finds their
window among twelve identical ones. The description is the only thing by which they decide
whether to install it. Tags are the only thing by which the store will ever assemble
shelves. While there are five products, the mess is invisible; at fifty it's irreversible,
because every card will need to be fixed after the fact, by hand.

**The rule.** A product is not rolled out without an icon, a description of at least 80
characters, and two tags, one of which is the product kind (`приложение`, `издание`,
`агент`, `демо`, `инструмент`; for a product with an English name — the English
equivalents `app`, `edition`, `agent`, `demo`, `tool`: the tag's language follows the
name's language). Checked by `tools/check_listing_meta.py`, which is also wired into
`deploy_edition.py` before rollout: a red card stops the rollout the same way a red
passport does.

**The icon must be produced by a command, or it will not exist.** `tools/make_icon.py
<sign> <path>` draws an icon from the Extella palette with no external package at all:
512×512, a geometric sign (a letter at 64 pixels is unreadable). While the icon was "a
separate job in an editor," it appeared zero times out of four.

**A tag is required on EVERY version (probe of 15 Aug 2026).** The platform started
responding `400 At least one tag is required` on `add-version-stream`, while the rollout
sent tags only when the listing was created — four products silently stopped updating.
The refusal was lost in the process: the stream wrapper explained it away as "the platform
didn't send a completion." **A response code != 200 is a response, not a dropped
connection:** print the reason verbatim.

**Where the metadata actually lives.** At the LISTING level: `name`, `description`, `tags`,
`has_icon`/`icon_ext`. The icon travels as a separate `icon` field in the same multipart
upload as `page` and `archive` (H10) — until 14 Aug 2026 the rollout didn't send this
field at all, hence the four grey squares.

**The desktop shortcut has NO metadata.** Probe: `POST /api/desktop/shortcut` with fields
`icon`, `description`, `tags` returns 200, and reading it back returns only `name` and
`url` — the platform silently swallows the rest. Requiring metadata on a shortcut is
pointless; the shortcut inherits its look from the listing.

**Careful with the desktop.** `POST /api/desktop-state` **overwrites the entire state**,
not appends to it: an empty body wipes the desktop (verified the expensive way — 11
shortcuts, folders and extensions disappeared and were restored from a backup taken a
minute earlier). Add a shortcut only through `POST /api/desktop/shortcut`. There is no
point deletion of a shortcut at the OS level (`DELETE /api/desktop/shortcut/{id}` → 404) —
remove it by writing the whole state, and **only with a fresh backup**.
