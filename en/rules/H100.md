# H100. ROUTE BEFORE RENDER: LOCAL ROAD FIRST, A DEAD ONE GETS REMEMBERED

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rules index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H100. ROUTE BEFORE RENDER: LOCAL ROAD FIRST, A DEAD ONE GETS REMEMBERED

**Source: the 1C Agent chat, measurements on a live test stand, 21 Sep 2026.**

**Measurement.** Opening the window without a direct road takes **46–59 s** to the first screen,
with a direct road — **13 s**. A single database read through the platform takes **11.5–12.0 s**,
over the direct road — **a fraction of a second**. The assembled page's size, 1.78 MB as a single
file, **was not the problem**: the seconds were spent in the network, not in parsing.

**Cost.** We optimized the wrong thing. The main source of delay turned out to be the route: the
panel talked to 1C through the platform even when the window was open on the very computer where 1C
runs.

**Rule.** Before optimizing the render — **measure which route the request is taking**. If the
product can work locally, the local path is tried first, and the result is shown to the person
("1C is connected · directly"). Four mandatory consequences, each from the measurement:

* **a failed attempt is remembered**: a road that failed to answer twice is set aside for 10
  minutes, otherwise on someone else's computer every read wastes time on a known-dead attempt;
* **a fresh port is not re-queried** (learned less than 60 s ago — take it as is);
* **the bootstrap is not requested twice at startup** — two races doubled the time to the first
  screen;
* **no copies and no reconnecting on every call**: a session with a queue instead of a
  process-per-call gave 0.1 s instead of seconds.
