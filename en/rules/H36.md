# H36. Product assets are served with no version in the address: people are left with the old one

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/en/rules/INDEX.md).

### H36. Product assets are served with no version in the address: people are left with the old one

**Probe of 21 Aug 2026.** The icon (`/icons/{lid}`) and the page (`/app-page/{lid}/`)
live at permanent addresses, with no version and no content fingerprint.

**How it shows up.** After an update, some people's desktops keep the old icon and the
old page. The server, meanwhile, serves the correct new bytes — verified by downloading.
The mismatch is entirely on the client side.

**Workaround.** Remove the icon and add it again; or reload with the cache cleared. It
resolves on its own once the cache's lifetime expires. The real fix is on the platform's
side: a version or a fingerprint in the resource address.

**A related point, from our own probe on 20 Aug 2026.** A heavy icon gets stuck in the
desktop's cache more reliably than a light one. Keeping the icon small — 256 points, tens
of kilobytes — is cheaper than explaining cache-clearing to people.
