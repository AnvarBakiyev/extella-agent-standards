# H92. The duplicate-name gate looks at the PRODUCT's experts, not the agent's whole inventory

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H92. The duplicate-name gate looks at the PRODUCT's experts, not the agent's whole inventory

The chat's fail-closed gate stopped the release of the construction company product because
`/api/agent-experts` returned 30 duplicate names on the agent (374 unique out of 404). But the
product's own catalog (19 business plus 2 diagnostic) is clean and unique — the 30 duplicates
were unrelated historical clutter on the same agent.

**Measured 06 Sep 2026 on our own healthy agent (Builder):** 380 records, 3 duplicate names,
**342 of 380 records with no `id`**. That is, duplicate names and records with no addressable
`id` are a **platform-wide condition**, present on every agent, including ours. Demanding "zero
duplicates across the whole agent" is meaningless and unachievable: deletion requires an `id`,
and most duplicates don't have one (canon `docs/INCIDENT_KV_SCOPE_SHADOWING.md`, correction
"deletion works, but not for the unnamed ones").

**Rule.** The standard forbids duplicates because they make resolution nondeterministic — but
that's about experts that the product **itself** calls. The duplicate-name gate checks the
**product's expert names** (its own catalog), not the agent's whole inventory. If the product's
catalog is unique, the product's resolution is deterministic, and unrelated historical
duplicates elsewhere do not block the release (§4.5: "duplicates — noted and moved on"). Want a
fully clean inventory — deploy the product to a fresh agent, don't wait for surgery on
undeletable platform records.
