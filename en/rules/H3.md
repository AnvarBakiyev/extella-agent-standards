# H3. Bundle assets are public — they cannot contain secrets

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/en/rules/INDEX.md).

### H3. Bundle assets are public — they cannot contain secrets

`GET /app-page/{lid}/{path}` is served **without a token**, with
`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. That is, everything sitting in the zip next to index is
accessible to anyone. Keys, private data, internal documents do not go in there.

The shortcut must end with a slash (`/app-page/{lid}/`): the proxy inserts `<base href>`,
and without the slash the assets' relative paths don't resolve — the classic symptom of
"a page with no styles."
