# H59. An expert's `global`: the flag is needed both on the record and IN THE READER'S REQUEST

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/en/rules/INDEX.md).

### H59. An expert's `global`: the flag is needed both on the record and IN THE READER'S REQUEST

**Resolving a contradiction, probe on 22 Aug 2026.** Three different products arrived at
three different conclusions about the same thing: one said a shared expert is visible to
everyone, another said it only runs from the scope that saved it last, a third said a
foreign agent doesn't find it at all and gets "expert not found." A direct probe
explains all three.

| how it is read | result |
|---|---|
| `expert/get` **with** `global: true` in the body, from four different scopes | all four see it |
| `expert/get` **without** `global: true`, from a foreign scope | HTTP 500, "Expert not found" |
| the same without the flag, from the owner's scope | sees it |

**Conclusion.** The shared-access flag on the record is half of the condition. The
second half: **the reader must ask for the shared search itself**. A client that doesn't
know this gets the refusal "the expert doesn't exist" — a wording that misleads: the
expert exists, what's missing is the flag in the request.

**What to do.** Check visibility not by a property of the record, but by live reading
**on behalf of every agent that needs the expert**, and with an explicit shared search.
A property of the record is not proof of availability.
