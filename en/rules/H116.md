# H116. RELEASING YOUR OWN WORK RUNS ON A LICENSE, NOT ON PERMISSION FOR EVERY VERSION

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rules index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H116. RELEASING YOUR OWN WORK RUNS ON A LICENSE, NOT ON PERMISSION FOR EVERY VERSION

**Measurement, 26 Sep 2026, bundle 1 (Open Notebook, Stirling-PDF, AFFiNE).** Over a day's work,
about ten real defects were found and fixed, two of them giving the user a SILENTLY WRONG result:
PDF cropping sent zero coordinates while the screen showed other values, and the "Download" button
saved someone else's file's bytes under someone else's name. Nothing changed in the store during
this time: a live `GET /api/listing/<id>` shows `pre.51`, `pre.31`, `pre.34`, all published more
than a day earlier. Writing experts to the live account was stopped as a standing external change,
and the release gate holds `PROVISIONING_SOURCE_DRIFT` — the local code is newer than what's
uploaded.

**Why permission for every version doesn't work.** It isn't verifiable: a person cannot sign off on
"seven adapter fixes, a hundred and five tests, archive SHA-256" — they can only agree to it.
**Confirmation a person is in no position to verify adds no safety, it only adds delay.** And it
comes back on the next version: the cost is paid every time, while the protection stays imaginary.

**What actually protects** is three things, each of them machine-enforced: the previous version
stays in the store; the expert's code is saved before being overwritten; after the write, a
read-back and byte-for-byte cross-check are performed, and any mismatch means a rollback.

**Rule.** A product has a license file, `РАЗРЕШЁН_ВЫПУСК.yaml`
(`templates/РАЗРЕШЁН_ВЫПУСК.yaml`): who issued it, when, which account, which listings, which
expert names, what kind of versions, a daily release ceiling, a rollback catalog. Within this
boundary, releasing proceeds without separate permission; `tools/check_release_license.py`
verifies it and answers "permitted" or names what's missing. Every release is logged as a line in
`ЖУРНАЛ_ВЫПУСКОВ.jsonl`.

**Outside the license — always a human:** the first paid release and a price change; someone
else's live product (`ВЕДУТ_ДРУГИЕ.txt`); deleting versions and listings (hiding is fine); expanding
`app_scopes` — the buyer's consent is what covers those; releasing on a red gate or without a saved
rollback.

**Consistent with the H-canon on freeze:** you can always change your own work, but with a trail and
a way back. The license is exactly that recorded trail proving the way back was planned in advance.
