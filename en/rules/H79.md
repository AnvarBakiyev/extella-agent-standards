# H79. A user's own free draft places itself on the desktop and stays deletable

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H79. A user's own free draft places itself on the desktop and stays deletable

**Measured 23 Aug 2026.** The product created an app and left it lying in the store: for the
tile to appear on the desktop, the human had to find the card and press "Buy & Deploy." Nobody
guessed this, and meanwhile the page said "already on the desktop" — a plain untruth.

**What was checked live.** `POST /api/purchase-stream/{version_id}` with an empty body on a
**own free unpublished** draft:

* answers 200 and sends a `webapp` event with `shortcut_id` — a tile on the desktop;
* after that `DELETE /api/listing/{id}` still answers **200**, the listing disappears.

That is, the earlier caution "a purchase makes a listing permanent" applies to **published**
listings (H26) and to other people's purchases, not to one's own draft. This should have been
checked earlier: because of the unverified assumption, the install step was done by hand for
years.

**The norm.** A product that creates an app for a human carries it **all the way to the
desktop**, not just to a card in the store. The full step is publish-stream (page + icon) plus
purchase-stream (tile), about 15 seconds together, within the deferral limit. If the second
step fails — say so directly and name the manual path, rather than promising a tile that
doesn't exist.
