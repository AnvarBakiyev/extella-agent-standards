# H98. THE DELIVERY PACKAGE MUST BE COHERENT: archive ⇄ installer ⇄ install.py at the root

### H98. THE DELIVERY PACKAGE MUST BE COHERENT: archive ⇄ installer ⇄ install.py at the root

**A census of the live storefront, 18 Sep 2026 (50 products, version composition from the store
API).** Four products cannot be installed at all, and not one of them says so; the buyer only
sees "The installer did not finish," with no reason:

| Problem | How many | Example |
|---|---|---|
| archive present, no `installer_expert` | 3 | Building on Extella (13 downloads), Personal Assistant (6), Lawyer (1) |
| `installer_expert` present, no archive | 1 | Recruiter (8 downloads) |
| neither a page nor an archive | 2 | Discussion Agent, Мияу |

**Cost: 28 downloads went to products that could not install.**

**Rule — four conditions, each from its own breakage.**

1. **An archive is declared — an `installer_expert` must exist.** Otherwise the platform has
   no one to call (Human Atlas: the page exists, a 32 MB archive exists, `device.run` rights are
   requested, there is no installer).
2. **An installer is declared — an archive must exist.** There's someone to call, nothing to
   deploy.
3. **`install.py` sits at the ROOT of the archive** (a reinforcement of B1). Lawyer has it — but
   one level deeper, inside an extra product folder. The installer can't find it, and no expert
   can work around this: the archive needs to be rebuilt.
4. **`install.py` is not nailed to one OS.** Human Atlas called `launchctl` with no branches and
   crashed on Linux with `No such file or directory: 'launchctl'` — the product could only
   install on a Mac. Caught by the presence of Mac-only calls with no `sys.platform`/
   `platform.system`/`os.name` check.

**And the main consequence, verified live on three systems: autostart doesn't decide the fate
of the install.** If a machine won't let you register a service (service accounts and
containers have no session), the installer must lay out the files, start the server, and PRINT
the start command — not crash. Measured 18 Sep 2026: with this branch, Human Atlas came up and
answered `{"ok": true}` both on Linux (a VPS) and on Windows (a test stand), where it used to
die.

**Capabilities (`kind: source`) are exempt from this rule** — they never have a page or a
per-device archive. The gate's first edition gave five false reds on them; a false red is just
as harmful as a miss, because a gate that lies gets turned off entirely.

**Machine check:** `tools/check_installable.py <product folder>` — in the general run and at the
publishing stage.
