# H111. TRANSLATION FRESHNESS IS CHECKED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

### H111. TRANSLATION FRESHNESS IS CHECKED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

**Measurement, 24 Sep 2026.** I made a correction about where the key comes from in the English
body of the section and **did not make it in the Russian one**. `check_translation` only kept a
fingerprint of the Russian text: the Russian hadn't changed, the fingerprint matched, the gate was
green. Two different sets of guidance under one name went into the assembled page — the Russian
one lied, the English one told the truth. Caught by eye while reviewing the assembled page, not by
a machine.

**Rule.** Next to the translation, **two** fingerprints are kept: of the original and of the
translation itself. If the Russian changes, the translation falls behind; if the English changes
without the fingerprints being rebuilt, the translation runs ahead. A translation without its own
fingerprint is declared unprotected: it can be edited unilaterally.

**The class is broader than translation.** A one-directional agreement check between two copies
doesn't see divergence in the other direction. Wherever two copies must match, both need a
fingerprint.
