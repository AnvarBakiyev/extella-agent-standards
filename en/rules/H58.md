# H58. An expired task is closed once, a late response does not revive it

Full corpus — `DEPLOY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; rule index — [rules/INDEX.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnvarBakiyev/extella-agent-standards/main/rules/INDEX.md).

### H58. An expired task is closed once, a late response does not revive it

**Probes from the same chat, 3–12 Aug 2026.** A late response arrived after the task's
lease had expired, and took part again in completing an already-closed task. The queue
and the panel looked stuck, even though the original task had long since expired.

**What to do.** An expired task is closed with a single receipt bearing an explicit
expiry reason; a late response does not replace it. Do not treat the absence of a
heartbeat DURING a synchronous call as the death of the worker — a long read has its own
time limit, separate from the heartbeat.

**A related case, from the same product.** A profile created through the shell did not
get a device binding: the card promised pinning at the first check, and the preparation
failed — the profile had been dead since the moment of creation. A promise to pin later
is acceptable only when the pinning actually happens; otherwise it is a half-finished
state that afterward cannot be told apart from a correct one.
